カナネコ Kananeko

How to play

Tap the buttons under the screen, or use a keyboard or a gamepad. Pick replies with the d-pad and A; in a kana quiz, press the d-pad toward the missing kana.

D-pad ←↑↓→ or WASD
A X or K
B Z or J
START Enter
SELECT Shift or Backspace

On iPhone, sound plays only with the ringer switch on.

About the game

Kananeko follows you through five places in town: the station, a street, a ramen counter, a convenience store, and an evening park. Everyone you meet speaks Japanese in kana; pick the friendly reply. After each conversation, a short quiz asks about the words you just read, and the day ends with a speed round.

It's a real Game Boy Color ROM, assembled entirely from TypeScript. Download the ROM to play it in any emulator, or read the source on GitHub.

Emulation by binjgb (MIT, Ben Smith). Kana glyphs from Misaki Gothic by Num Kadoma.
